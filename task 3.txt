Milestone 3 :  Service Catalog 

Activity 1: Service Catalog for placing order and get Approval

Click on All >> Search for “ Service Catalog ”.
Click on Service Catalog >> click on Hardware.
Scroll a bit to find the “ Standard Laptop ”  click on that.
After that click on “ Order Now ” to place the request.

Now we will see the Order status 
In that click on the Request Number 

It will open the Requested number record.
Scroll down Click on the Approvers Section.

Right Click on the Record to see config in that Click on the Approve.

Then click on the Requested item Section 
Open that Requested Item record

Scroll down to the “ Catalog tasks ” Section  
 Open the record to see the updated status of the record with Short description and Assigned group.
