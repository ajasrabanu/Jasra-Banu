Milestone 2: Flow Assignment
Activity 1: Flow Assignment to Standard Laptop Service Catalog

STEPS:
1. Open ServiceNow.
2. Click on All >> Search for Maintain Items.
3. Select Maintain Items.
4. Under the Name field, search for 'Standard Laptop'
5. Select that record.
6. Under that record, select Process Engine.
7. Remove the remaining automations.
8. Add Flow.
9. Add the flow created with name 'Standard Laptop Task'.
10. Click on the menu to Save the record.
